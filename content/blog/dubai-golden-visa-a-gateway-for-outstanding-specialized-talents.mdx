### **Dubai golden visa requirements and** **Eligibility**�

Dubais [**Golden Visa for specialized talents**](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/business-investment-immigration-visa.html) is designed for individuals who bring exceptional value to the UAEs growth story. Unlike general residency permits, this visa category recognizes professionals, innovators, and achievers across medicine, science, culture, sports, and technology. Each category has its own set of requirements that applicants must fulfill.

**Doctors & Scientists**

- Must hold official **approval from the Ministry of Health** to practice in the UAE (for doctors).

- Scientists may require a **recommendation from the Emirates Scientists Council** or recognition through the prestigious Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum Award for Academic Excellence.

- A valid **passport** and **health insurance** (minimum one year, renewable) are mandatory.  
    

**Innovators & Creative Professionals**

- **Artists, writers, and cultural leaders** need an endorsement from the Department of Culture & Arts in their emirate.

- **Inventors** should present a recommendation or proof of recognition from the Ministry of Economy.

- Applicants must also provide a passport copy and valid health insurance coverage.  
    

**Executive Leader**s

- Candidates must hold a **recognized university degree**.

- At least **five years of senior-level experience** is required.

- Proof of a **monthly salary of AED 50,000 or more,** along with a valid employment contract.

- Passport and insurance documents are compulsory.  
    

**Specialists & Athletes**

- Professionals in rare or high-demand scientific fields should provide a **specialized degree or certification** recognized by the UAE authorities.

- Athletes must secure a **recommendation from the General Sports Authority or a regional sports council**.

- Passport and insurance remain standard requirements.  
    

**PhD Holders & Engineers/Scientists**

- Holders of a **PhD in priority disciplines** approved by the Ministry of Education are eligible.

- Specialists in engineering, biotechnology, artificial intelligence, big data, electronics, or genetics must show **academic credentials and a valid work contract** in their field.

- A passport and health insurance are also required.  
    

**Outstanding Students**

- **High School Achievers:** Top-ranking students with a Ministry of Education recommendation may qualify for a **5-year** [**Golden Visa**](http://xiphiasimmigration.com/business-investment-immigration-visa.html), extendable if enrolled in longer study programs like medicine. They can also sponsor parents and siblings under specific conditions.

- **University Students:** Those maintaining a **GPA of 3.8 or above** at a recognized university may obtain a **10-year Golden Visa**. Financially capable students can sponsor parents, siblings, spouse, and children.
