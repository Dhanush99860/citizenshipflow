Popularly known as the Spice Island because of its myriad locally grown spices, Grenada is one of the waterfronts in all the Caribbean. Sailing, diving, great restaurants, amazing beaches, and a calendar of interesting festivities add to the many reasons why Grenada has become a preferred destination for many high-net-worth investors.

## Requirements of Grenada Citizenship by Investment:

Grenada's second Passport by Investment Program was launched in 2013 under the regulations of the **Grenada Citizenship****��by Investment Act.**

To qualify for citizenship in Grenada, applicants must fulfill one of the investment options in addition to meeting the following criteria:

1. Be of outstanding character.
2. Hold no criminal record.
3. Have excellent health.
4. Have a high personal net worth.

### **Investment Options:**

The investment options for the [Grenada Citizenship by Investment](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/grenada-citizenship-by-investment-immigration.html) program are as follows:

1. **National Transformation Fund Donation**

Applicants can contribute a minimum of US$150,000 to Grenada�����s National Transformation Fund. The minimum amount is applicable to the main applicant only. For a family of up to four members, the requirement is estimated at US$200,000. An extra contribution of US$25,000 has to be made for each additional dependent. Donations are transferred in two installments.

2\. **Real Estate Investment**

- Mount Cinnamon Shares

Applicants may invest a minimum of US$350,000 for a family of up to four (main applicant, spouse, and two qualifying dependents). An extra US$25,000 will be required for each additional dependent. Mount Cinnamon covers the following costs for the main applicant and spouse: due diligence, an investment fee, government and application fees, passport and Oath of Allegiance fees, local escrow fees, and related VAT and bank charges (up to US$3,500).

Dependents are required to pay the fees listed above and applicable charges. Getting the opportunity to own shares of Mount Cinnamon is an invitation to become a citizen of Grenada, you can begin a long and fruitful relationship with the island and its culture and contribute to its future.

- Mount Cinnamon Freehold

The new limited release of just 25 whole-ownership condominiums will be available at US$450,000. Ownership of these 484 square feet of fully furnished units is mandated for a period of 5 years after which the property can be sold or retained. In either circumstance, units would remain as a part of the hotel rental pool.

## **Advantages of investing in Grenada**

Following are the few benefits of Grenadian Citizenship by Investment Program

1. Fast processing within 4 months.
2. Inclusion of dependent children under 26.
3. Inclusion of dependent parents over 55.
4. No physical residency requirements.
5. No requirement to travel to Grenada during the application process.
6. No interview, education, or managerial experience is required.
7. Visa-free travel access to 100 countries, which includes Europe's Schengen zone and China.
8. E-2 treaty signed between Grenada and the United States. Which allows citizens of Grenada to operate a substantial business in the U.S.
9. No tax on worldwide income.
