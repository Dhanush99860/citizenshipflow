**What are Skilled Worker Manitoba Streams?**

The skilled worker categories work through an "Expression of Interest" system, in this process, an immigrant or applicant need to complete a series of questions which are online but through this process, immigrant don�����t need to upload document before submitting a profile to the EOI pool. EOI pool receives many eligible application and profile and after submitting a profile each candidate receive a score based on the answer provided. The score is generated through the Manitoba immigration points calculator. If your profile is be highest scoring among all, you may be invited to submit an application to the **Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program****.** In these streams, all applications are accepted from qualified international & foreign workers who are currently working temporarily in Manitoba and have been offered full-time and permanent jobs with Manitoba employers.

Manitoba's work experience pathway is for those working in Manitoba with temporary work permit and who wants to apply through the Manitoba Province Nominee Program, but employer-direct recruitment pathways is just different, through this pathways employer can apply outside of Canada for MPNP.

**What is a Skilled Worker Overseas Stream?**

To access a candidate a point-based system is used in Manitoba Skilled Worker Overseas Streams, in this process candidate may be outside of Canada. In Skilled Worker Overseas Stream application accepts qualified skilled workers who can show a strong relation and connection to the province through experience of education and employment, friends and family, or by invitation of the MPNP and need to show a score of 60 points which is a minimum points and based on five eligibility factors. The minimum 60 points are defined on the basis of age, work experience, language, adaptability, and education.

**Manitoba Express Entry** is a pathway of skilled worker overseas stream, Manitoba Express Entry pathways work for those who are eligible under another MPNP stream and according to Manitoba Express Entry requirement.
