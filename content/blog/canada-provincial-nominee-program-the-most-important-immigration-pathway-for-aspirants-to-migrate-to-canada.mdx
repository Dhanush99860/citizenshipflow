What is Provincial Nominee Program?

Provincial Nominee Program is one of the pathways through which interested migrants can relocate to a chosen Province in Canada. The ten provinces in Canada are actively participating in the Provincial nomination Program. These provinces have the right to choose the best candidate to fit in to their province. Such chosen candidates will be awarded nomination from the province which would help further increase the score of the candidate and fortify the individual�����s dream to relocate to Canada. Once, the candidate is nominated, he/she can then apply for ITA with IRCC. IRCC would then select candidates through a draw and sends ITAs. Once the ITA is received, the successful candidate and move on to apply for permanent residency with Canada.

Provincial nominee program is points based and was started in the year 1998. The program invites both skilled professionals and business investors to the province. The successful applicants will need to live in the said province for a given period as decided by the province. Provinces like New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island, Newfound land are major attractors for aspirants under Canada PNP category. In the recent past, Alberta, Manitoba and Saskatchewan are seeing a raise in the number of applications. A major benefit of PNP is the amount of time it takes to process the application. The aspirant can receive his or her nomination within 6 months of filing and in the next, the aspirant might well receive his or her ITA. Just as other programs, this immigration program too allows main applicant, spouse and dependant children to apply together for the program.
