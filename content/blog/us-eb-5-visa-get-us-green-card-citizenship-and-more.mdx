The United States **EB-5 visa program** is a popular and effective way for high-net-worth individuals and their families to obtain a Green Card in the United States. The EB-5 program is specifically designed to attract foreign investment in the US economy and create jobs for American citizens. The EB-5 visa program is one of the fastest ways to obtain a Green Card in the United States.

Once the investor's petition is approved, investors and their families can obtain a conditional Green Card within a matter of months. Also, the US is a diverse, multi-religious, and pluralistic democracy similar to India and with a robust and strong economy making it a hot destination for immigrating and living the American Dream for Indians.

## Pathways under the US EB-5 visa program:

There are two pathways to an EB-5 Immigrant Investor Visa - Direct EB-5 investment requires an investment in a real operating business that results in the creation of 10 full-time jobs. The I-526 approval for a direct investment takes much less time than the I-526 filed through a Regional Center. A regional center EB-5 investment typically has a 5- to 9-year lock-in period while a Direct EB-5 investment can be withdrawn at any time by the investor.

## Benefits of a Green Card:

- Ability to renew your Green Card every 10 years.
- Travel to and from the United States more easily compared to other Visa holders.
- Freedom to travel and live anywhere within the territory of the US.
- Eligible to receive benefits such as social security or education assistance. Green Card holders can also apply for government-sponsored financial aid in education.
- Green card holders have greater job opportunities compared to those on a work Visa and apply for jobs that involve security clearances and work for the government.

## **Requirements of the US EB-5:**

Investors must invest in a new commercial enterprise that was established: After 29th November 1990; or On or before 29th November 1990, that was purchased but the existing business must be restructured to result in a new commercial enterprise, or expanded through investment to achieve a 40% increase in net worth or the number of employees. The new commercial enterprise can be defined as a for-profit activity, formed for the lawful ongoing conduct of business.
