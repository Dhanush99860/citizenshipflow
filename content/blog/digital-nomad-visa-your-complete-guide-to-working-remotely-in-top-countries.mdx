A **digital nomad visa** is not just a permission slip to stay in another country; its a way to combine work, travel, and lifestyle legally. With a digital nomad visa, remote workers and freelancers can live in another country for months or even years while still doing their jobs online.

### **General Digital Nomad Visa Requirements**

While each country sets its own rules, most **digital nomad visas** require:

- Proof of stable remote income

- Health insurance valid in the destination country

- A valid passport with sufficient validity

- No criminal record

- Sometimes, the inclusion of family members is allowed.
