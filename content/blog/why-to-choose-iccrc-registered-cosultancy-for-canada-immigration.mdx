ICCRC (Immigration Consultants of Canada Regulatory Council) is the national regulatory officials assigned by the Government of Canada to prevent fraud for an individual who is looking for Canada Immigration guidance and representation from immigration consultants. Governmental law expects that immigration consultant in Canada or abroad, who give Canadian immigration services by taking a fee, must be enrolled with ICCRC and authorized as Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultants (RCICs).

So, before you decide to look for the assistance of a consultant for Canada immigration, you need to check the authenticity of the service provider. By choosing ICCRC company/ICCRC agents you can avoid fake visas and visa rejection face.

Here are a couple of tips to check if the immigration consultant is genuine:

1.The first thing you need to check is the immigration consultant is ICCRC (Immigration Consultants of Canada Regulatory Council) registered or not.

2.The consultant you chose must have a proper office set-up. You can also check, from how long they are providing the immigration service.

3.Read their company terms and conditions completely.
