## What is a Golden Visa?

A Golden Visa is a long-term residency visa issued by the UAE government, enabling foreign investors, entrepreneurs, skilled professionals, and talented individuals to reside, work, or study in the UAE without requiring a local sponsor. It offers **5 to 10 years of renewable residency**, depending on eligibility.
