### **Categories Applicable:**

**1\. Short-Term stream**

It enables skilled workers to live and work in Australia.

Valid stay years: 2 years minimum. Extendable to 4 years (if International Trade Obligation (ITO) applies).

**2\. Medium-Term stream**

It enables Skilled Workers to live and work in Australia.

Valid stay years: up to 4.

**3\. Labor agreement stream**

This visa is applicable to skilled workers nominated by Labor Agreement employers. This agreement must be between the Australian Government and employers. Valid stay years: up to 4.

**4\. Subsequent entrant**

Family members of subclass 457 and 482 visa holders can apply for TSS visa separately.

Valid stay years: As per visa validity.

Processing time: Immediate

### **Requirements for Australia Skilled 482 visa:**

The Applicant must-

1. Be nominated by an approved sponsor for a skilled position.
2. Have the appropriate abilities to do the job.
3. Have English proficiency.
