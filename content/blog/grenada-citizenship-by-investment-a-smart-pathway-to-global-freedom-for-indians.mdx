- **Processing Time**: Just 36 months

- **Visa-Free Travel**: 145+ countries including Schengen, UK, China

- **No Residency Requirement**

- **Citizenship for Life**: Passed down to future generations

## Investment Options for Grenada Citizenship

To qualify for [Grenadas golden visa](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/grenada-citizenship-by-investment-immigration.html), you must choose one of the following:

### A) Government Donation

- **Amount**: $200,000 (single applicant)

- **Type**: Non-refundable contribution to the National Transformation Fund  
    

### B) Real Estate Investment

- **Amount**: $220,000 minimum in a government-approved project

- **Holding Period**: 5 years

- Ideal if youre looking for ROI through rentals or resale
