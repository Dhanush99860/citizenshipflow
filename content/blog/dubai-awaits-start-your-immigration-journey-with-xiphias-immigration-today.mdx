Dubai's immigration procedures for tourists, residents and workers are overseen by the General directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA). Dubai visa categories are Property owner visa, tourist visas, Work visa, Investor visa, Retiree visa, residency visas (such as Green and Golden Visas) and Work permits. Eligible nationalities also benefit from a visa-on-arrival option. For added convenience, Dubai Airports provides Smart Gate system to expedite passport control.

Dubai business visa permits individuals willing to move to Dubai UAE for professional activities such as attending meetings, conferences, or exploring investment opportunities. The Options are short-term visit visas (valid for 30 or 90 days) as well as longer-term multiple-entry visas, which are valid for up to six months. [Dubai working visa](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/dubai-business-investor-visa.html) allow any foreign individual to legally live, work and settle in Dubai. It is typically arranged through employer sponsorship and involves several steps governed by UAE labour and immigration authorities.
