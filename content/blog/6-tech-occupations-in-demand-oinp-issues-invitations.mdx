#### **What does the OINP trend suggest?**

Going by the statistics, if we glance at the present trend within the Ontario PNP Tech attracts, we can clearly substantiate that the amount of invitations has increased rapidly with a decreasing CRS (Comprehensive Ranking System) score. So this is high time for all professionals whose profile falls under this NOC code (National Occupational Classification) with relevant experience.

#### **What is Ontario PNP Tech Stream?**

Ontario PNP Tech Stream audits and investigates the qualified profiles from the Express Entry pool among the IT-related occupations. Every one of the profiles and applications is deliberately assessed and at that point the provincial govt. declares the OINP Tech Express Entry welcome round draws pursued by the Notification of Interest.

The program is explicitly designed to pull in IT-skilled Professionals from all over the globe to fill the labor shortage in Canada. The figures predict that around 220,000 Skilled laborers will be required in the Information and Communication Technology sector of Canada by 2021.
