### **Eligibility for the federal economic immigration program is a must:**

The Canada Express entry system isn't open for all. Only those applicants who meet the eligibility criteria of the federal economic immigration program can apply. These are - the Federal Skilled Worker Program, the Federal Skilled Trades Program, and The Canadian Experience Class.

### **Canadian job offer is not compulsory:**

Some of you might be under the perception that to apply for the Canada PR Visa through the express entry system, you need to have a job offer from a Canadian employer.

The fact is that a Canadian job offer is not mandatory. If you have a Canadian job offer in hand then it boosts up your chances of selection by adding extra points to CRS.

### **Fact about eligible occupation list:**

Currently, there's no eligible occupation list as such. The candidate just needs 1 year of experience in a skilled occupation within the last 10 years. Candidate needs to know about the National Occupational Classification (NOC) codes. It is assigned based on a skill type a level of expertise.

### **The language test is mandatory for Canada Express Entry:**

To apply for a Canada Permanent Resident Visa through express entry, the candidate needs to pass the language proficiency test. The candidate needs to prove language proficiency in English and/or French language.

When you are creating your express entry profile you should ensure that you mention the only truth. Keep in mind that on the off chance that you lie, then you will have to face serious consequences. So whatever information you mention must be true.
