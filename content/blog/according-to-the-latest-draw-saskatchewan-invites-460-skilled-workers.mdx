Applicants who have not submitted their profile in the Express Entry Pool can apply under the Occupations In-demand sub-category. The 28th November attract invited 260 up-and-comers under the Occupations In-demand sub-category.

You do not need a job offer from Saskatchewan to apply under either the Occupations In-demand sub-categories or the Express Entry. You must have at least 1 year of work experience in an eligible skilled occupation that is related to your field of study.

Since 25th September, the SINP has led 15 draws issuing 5,689 invitations to both Occupations In-demand candidates & Express Entry. To apply under either the Occupations In-demand sub-categories or Express Entry, you should initially present an EOI to the SINP. Qualified EOIs are scored out of an aggregate of 100.
