## What is Canada's Global Talent Stream?

The Canada Global Talent Stream is a program designed to help Canadian employers hire highly skilled foreign workers for positions that cannot be filled by Canadian citizens or permanent residents. It provides employers with a faster and more efficient way to hire specialized foreign workers. The program is managed by Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC) and Immigration, Refugees, and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). It also provides the option to obtain a [Canada PR.](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/immigrationlanding/enquiry-usa.html)

The program is divided into two categories: Category A and Category B. The Global Talent Stream prioritizes occupations that are in high demand in Canada, such as information technology, engineering, and science.

## What are the Canada global talent stream requirements & eligibility?

The main requirements and eligibility of the Canada Global Talent Stream are:

1\. You may be eligible for Category A of the Global Talent Stream if you have been referred to the Global Talent Stream by one of the Stream's designated partners and if you are hired as an individual with unique and specialized talent.

You may be eligible for Category B of the Global Talent Stream if you are being hired as a highly-skilled foreign worker to fill positions in in-demand occupations found on the Global Talent Occupations List. A referral is not required to be eligible for Category B. Also, the position must pay a salary equal to or above the prevailing wage for the position.

## How to apply for the Canada Global Talent stream?

As a foreign worker, you cannot apply directly to the Global Talent Stream. Only a Canadian employer can apply to the Global Talent Stream, and the foreign worker(s) they wish to hire is listed on the application. This means that you should first be able to land a job offer from a Canadian employer who qualifies for the program. You may check the Global Talent occupation list and the designated partners.
