### **What is Canada's Permanent Residency?**

**Canada Permanent Residency (PR)** allows skilled individuals and families to live, work, and settle in Canada permanently. PR holders enjoy access to free healthcare, education, and career opportunities, along with the freedom to apply for Canadian citizenship after meeting eligibility requirements.

## �����**Canada Permanent Resident Eligibility Criteria**

To qualify for PR, you must typically:

- Have **1+ year of skilled work experience**

- Score **CLB 7 or higher** in English or French  
    

- Complete an **Educational Credential Assessment (ECA)**

- Pass a **medical and background check**

- Show **proof of funds**

- Score well on the **Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS)** (for Express Entry)
