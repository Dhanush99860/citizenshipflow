# Skilled migration to Australia : What Are Your Options?

Planning for an [Australia PR Visa](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/australia-immigration-visa-consultants.html) ? When planning your move to Australia, the most popular and reliable options for Indians are through skilled migration visas:

- Subclass 189 (Skilled Independent Visa)  A points-tested permanent residency visa that does not require state nomination or employer sponsorship. Perfect for highly skilled professionals.

- Subclass 190 (Skilled Nominated Visa)  Requires nomination by an Australian state or territory government. Offers quicker processing and additional points.

- Subclass 491 (Skilled Work Regional Visa  Provisional) A temporary visa (5 years) that allows you to live and work in designated regional areas, with a pathway to permanent residency.

### Who Can Apply? Australia PR Visa Eligibility in 2025

[Eligibility for an Australia PR visa](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/blogs/what-are-the-australia-business-visa-categories/) is primarily points-based (for subclasses 189, 190, and 491). As an Indian applicant, youll need to meet the following common requirements:

- Age: Ideally below 45 years at the time of application.

- Skills Assessment: Your occupation must be on the Skilled Occupation List (SOL) and assessed by a relevant authority.

- English Proficiency: Demonstrated through IELTS, PTE, or TOEFL.

- Work Experience: Relevant professional experience in your nominated occupation.

- Points Test: Minimum 65 points, though higher points increase your chances.

- Health & Character Checks: Mandatory for you and included dependents.
