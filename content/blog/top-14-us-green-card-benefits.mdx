For one to be qualified for US citizenship, all immigrants must have a green card and must be resident for at least 5 years before applying for citizenship. There are five [US employment-based resident visas](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/usa-corporate-immigration-services.html)�**available- EB1, EB2, EB3, EB4, and EB5.** Through every one of them, you become eligible to obtain a [US Green Card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_card).

## **Benefits of US Green Card:**

1\. You can live in any of the 50 states of the US.

2\. Able to enter and leave the U.S. at any time.

3.No worries about the expiry of your permitted stay. It is renewable every 10 years.

4.No restrictions on sponsorship to work in the US, job type, or weekly working hours. A lot of job opportunities.

5\. You can apply for government funding sponsorship to pursue education.

6\. School, college, and university tuition fees are less compared to foreigners.

7\. You can initiate your own business or even run a corporation.

8\. If you've been working for 40 quarters, about 10 years, then after retirement you're eligible for Social Security benefits (Retirement, Disability, Survivors, and dependants).

9\. Unlike a temporary residence permit holder, no changes in immigration rules need to be worried.

10\. You are legally able to sponsor permanent status for your spouse and unmarried children under the age of 21.

11\. Most banks insist that a non-citizen has a green card or long-term visa as a prerequisite for sanctioning a mortgage and some banks have preferential green card-holders interest rates. This makes it simple for green card holders, even at a reduced interest rate, to get home loans.

12\. Some of the U.S. states require you to carry a green card to get a license to have some employment like an insurance agent or real estate agent. Having a green card takes away barriers to holding those positions.

13\. Like American citizens, Green Card holders can buy a vehicle, own property, and get a firearms license.

14\. You can contribute to political campaigns.
