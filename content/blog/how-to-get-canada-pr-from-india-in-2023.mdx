## How to apply for Canada PR from India?

Given below are the main steps to file your Canada PR visa application from India.

1\. **Determine eligibility and check requirements:** As mentioned above, there are various immigration programs available for Canada PR. You will need to choose the one you are eligible for and check requirements such as language tests, work experience, etc.

2. **Submit an application:** based on the requirements of the chosen immigration program.

3\. **Submit documents:** provide all the genuine and required documents such as educational certificates, language test results, and work experience among others.

4\. **Additional documents/proof:** proof of funds to be able to stay in Canada, medical checkup, and police verification record.

5\. **Mandatory examination of documents by an immigration officer:** Additional information may be asked and you need to be ready to provide the required information.

6\. **Confirmation of your PR status:** you will receive a COPR (Confirmation of Permanent Residence) card.

7\. Apply for your PR card and travel to Canada.
