## Benefits of Grenada Citizenship:

- With a Grenada passport and Grenada citizenship, individuals can enjoy visa-free travel or visa-on-arrival access to 145 countries, including China, the United Kingdom, the Schengen Area in Europe, and other top business and lifestyle destinations.
- Grenada is the only Caribbean country with a Citizenship program that holds an E-2 Investor Visa treaty with the United States. This treaty enables Grenadian citizens to apply for a US e-2 visa.
- Family members can be included under one application which includes:
    - Spouse
    - Parents, and grandparents (No age limit for parents and grandparents)
    - Dependent children up to the age of 30
    - Newborn children of the main applicant
    - Unmarried siblings (with no children) of the main applicant and spouse
- The fast and efficient application process of approximately three months from submission.
