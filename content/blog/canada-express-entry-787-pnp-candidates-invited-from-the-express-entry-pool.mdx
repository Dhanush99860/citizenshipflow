APRIL 14, 2022 11:42:11 AM

Canada invites 787 applicants from the Express Entry pool to apply for permanent residence. These are candidates who had been previously nominated through a Provincial Nominees Program (PNP) and they had a minimum CRS (Comprehensive Ranking Score) of 782. The score is a combination of base score of 182 and 600 points added to the score on receipt of nomination.

In the previous invitation round, Immigration, refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) invited 919 PNP candidates with scores of at least 785.
