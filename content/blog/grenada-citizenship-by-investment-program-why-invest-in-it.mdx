Grenada is a small island in the Caribbean, with fast economic growth and political stability. Staying in Grenada is like living in heaven with no criminal record and a high standard of living for all citizens of Grenada. Our article will help you understand the details of the Grenada Citizenship by Investment Program that the country offers.

![Grenada Investment Migration](/images/blogs/grenada-citizenship-by-investment-program-why-invest-in-it/Grenada-CBI.png)

Grenada is an island country in the Caribbean Sea made up of the main island and six smaller islands, with a consolidated size of 344 square kilometers. Grenada passport holders are offered free VISA travel to 141 countries like the UK, the Schengen Area, China, Brazil, and other key business and lifestyle destinations

Grenada is one of the primary exporters of a few distinct spices, for example, Cinnamon, mace, and ginger, and is the second biggest maker of nutmeg, whose picture is a part of the nation's flag. This is how Grenada named "The Island of Spice". The beauty and beaches of Grenada attract many people to visit and settle in Grenada.

### **Grenada Citizenship by Investment Program offers:**

- No requirement to physically live in the nation.
- No need to travel to Grenada while your application is being processed.
- No work experience or education requirement and no interview.
- Enjoy dual citizenship
- Visa-free travel to more than 141 countries like the UK, Schengen Area, China, and Brazil.
- Grenada citizens can start a business in the US and become residents under the E-2 Investor Visa program
- Dependent children under 25 and Dependent parents can be included in the application.
- No tax on worldwide income for Grenada citizens.
- Citizenship for lifelong with a right to work and live in Grenada.
- Right to pass citizenship to the next generation.

### **Benefits of Grenada Citizenship by Investment program:**

- Globally recognized citizenship by investment program built up in the laws and guidelines of Grenada.
- Can include various family members in the Application.
- Quick process.
- No need for an interview.
- No language necessity.
- No past business experience necessity
- No need to live in Grenada before, during, or after the application.

### **Eligibility:**

1\. Must have a net worth of:

- US $200,000 is required for NTF(National Transformation Fund)
- US $350,000 is required for investment in real estate.

2\. Should provide proof of the source of funds

3\. Should have no criminal record.

4\. Should be physically fit.
