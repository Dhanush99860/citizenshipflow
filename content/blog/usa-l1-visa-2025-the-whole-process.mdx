### **What Is the USA L1 Visa?**

The [USA L1 visa](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/RealEstate/Properties/usa) is designed for employees of multinational companies who are relocating to the U.S. to work in a managerial, executive, or specialized knowledge role.

How long can you stay on an L1 visa? It depends on your category:

- New office setup: 1 year initially.

- L1A visa: Up to 7 years total (initial 3 years + extensions).

- L1B visa: Up to 5 years total.

Since the L1 visa duration of stay in the US is limited, many applicants eventually transition to permanent residency, taking advantage of the L1s dual intent flexibility.

## **L1 Visa Processing Time**

The **L1 visa processing** time varies, but heres a general idea:

- Standard processing: A few weeks to several months.

- Premium processing: Around 15 calendar days.

The process involves filing Form I-129 with USCIS, receiving the I-797 approval notice, and completing the visa stamping process at a U.S. consulate.

For businesses needing urgent transfers, XIPHIAS Immigration often recommends premium processing to cut down delays.

## **H1B to L1A Conversion in USA**

Already in the U.S. on an H1B? Switching through an [**H1B to L1A conversion**](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/blogs/dreaming-of-the-u-s-l1a-and-l1b-visa-guide-for-indian-professionals/) in USA could be a game-changer.

- To qualify, you must have worked for your employer abroad for at least 1 of the last 3 years.

- Unlike H1B, the L1A avoids lottery restrictions and allows for dual intent, making it easier to plan for permanent residency.

This pathway is especially popular for professionals seeking managerial positions and long-term relocation opportunities.
