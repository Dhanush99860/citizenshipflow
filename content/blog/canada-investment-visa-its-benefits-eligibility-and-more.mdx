#### **The requirement to live in a specific Canadian State or Territory?**

This depends on the specific visa type. If you have selected a Central Immigration Plan to immigrate to Canada under the Central Investor Division, you can settle anywhere in the country. On the other hand, if you have taken a Provincial Constitutional Plan (PNP) or Quebec Investor Immigration Program (QIIP), you have to settle in a particular province.
