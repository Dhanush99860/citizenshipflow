#### FAQs:

#### 1\. What is the process to be followed for the Canada Startup Visa Program?

- Secure a commitment from a designated organization: You need to get a letter of support from a designated organization in Canada.
- Submit an application: The application requires you to provide your personal information, business plan, and other supporting documents.
- Processing: The processing time for a Canada Start-up Visa application can vary, but it can take a few months. During this time, you may be asked to provide additional information or attend an interview.
- Approval: If your application is approved, you will receive a letter of introduction that allows you to apply for a work permit. Once you receive the work permit, you can move to Canada and start building your business.
