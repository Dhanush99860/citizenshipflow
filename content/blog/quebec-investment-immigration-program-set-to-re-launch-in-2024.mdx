# Introduction

Exciting developments are in store for individuals aspiring to secure Canadian residency through Quebec's Immigrant Investor Programme (QIIP), set to resume operations on **January 1, 2024.** After a temporary suspension in 2019, the QIIP has undergone substantial revisions. However, prospective investors must familiarize themselves with the significant changes incorporated into the program's eligibility criteria.

![Quebec Investor Immigration program](/images/blogs/quebec-investment-immigration-program-set-to-re-launch-in-2024/Canada-RBI.png)

## Key Revisions in the QIIP (Quebec Investment Migration Program)

**French Language Proficiency:** The revamped QIIP now introduces a notable language proficiency requirement. Applicants are required to demonstrate a high intermediate proficiency in spoken French, equivalent to level 7.

**Residency Requirement:** An obligatory residency period in Quebec is now part of the criteria. Within two years of obtaining a work permit, the principal applicant must have lived in Quebec for a minimum of 6 months. Furthermore, either the principal applicant or their spouse/de facto spouse must have resided in Quebec for an additional period of at least 6 months.
