The United States has always been a popular destination for businessmen and professionals looking to realise their American dream by establishing a company. The Immigrant Investor Program, also known as EB-5, is the most effective way to realise one's ambition. The Fifth Employment-Based Preference, also known as the Fifth Employment-Based Preference, is a great way for investors and their immediate families to obtain a green card and become lawful permanent residents of the United States. An investor only needs money to make his or her dream a reality.

Finding the right investment option and navigating the logistics of making the investment that will lead to a US Green Card, on the other hand, is not as simple as it sounds. The first critical choice that an investor must make is whether to invest directly or through an EB-5 Regional Center. Let us explain the key difference between a Direct investment and an investment through a Regional Center.

There are two pathways to EB-5 Immigrant Investor Visa - Direct EB-5 investment requires an investment in a real operating business that results in creation of 10 full-time jobs. A direct investment has been the fastest way to obtain a EB-5 visa. The I-526 approval for a direct investment takes much less time that I-526 filed through a Regional Center takes. A regional center EB-5 investment typically has a 5- to 9-year lock in period. A Direct EB-5 investment, on the other hand, can be withdrawn at any time by the investor.

In the Direct Investment pathway an investor has the option of developing a venture based on their innovative idea or else invest in a real operating business. However, the responsibility of creating and maintaining full-time jobs for qualified employees, that should not include family members, is a challenge.

#### A Summary of the key differences between Eb-5 Direct Investment or Investment through Regional Centers

| Advantages | Direct EB-5 Investment | Regional Center EB-5 Investment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type of Investment | Direct Investment | Indirect Investment |
| Source of funds | Investor has to provide proof of funds | Investor has to provide proof of funds |
| Job Creation & No. of Jobs | 10 Direct Jobs | Direct/Indirect jobs, based on the formula created by the Regional Center for a particular industry |
| Ownership | Investor owns equity | Investor gives a loan for 5 years with an exit option |
| Management | Investor manages the company | Regional Center appoints a a management company |
| Control | Investor has full control over the project | Investor is one of the many investors in the project. Hence has minimum control |
| Experience | Investor must be experienced to operate the business in the opted industry | Investor does not need to have any experience in the project or the industry |
| Time Lines | 12 to 18 months | 18 to 24 months |
| Return on Investment | Investor get higher rate of return owing to direct management and control | Investor lower rate of return due to low risks in projects |
