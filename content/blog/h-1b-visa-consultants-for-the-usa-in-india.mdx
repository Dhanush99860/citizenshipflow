Highly eligible Professionals in the field of architecture, engineering, arts, laws, accounting, mathematics, sciences, medicine, education or businesses are applicable to go to the [USA](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/blog/H-1B-visa-consultants-for-the-USA-in-India.html) on H-1B visa. You need to have a Sponsorship to be eligible for this visa. This is a non-immigrant visa and a dual intent visa. Dual intent visa means that your temporary work visa can be converted into a Permanent Resident Visa (Green Card). There is no restriction to work under multiple US employees. However, multiple employment would need you to have each employer I-129 petition form as approval.

## **What are the requirements for the H-1B visa?**

1. Applicant should hold US bachelors or masters or an equivalent degree.
2. You must have Job offer letter for a professional occupation level.
3. You require demonstrating your knowledge.
4. 2 years of work experience.

## **How long H-1B visa holder able to stay in the US?**

1. Stay time is about 3 years.
2. Additional 3-year extension.
3. Maximum 10 years of extension for a specific type of work.
