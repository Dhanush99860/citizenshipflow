Employment rises for workers more than 55.

The number of occupations for laborers more than 55 years increased by 31,000. The unemployment rate among such specialists dropped by 0.2% to 4.7%.

Between October 2018 and October 2019, the number of jobs for laborers more than 55 expanded by 187,000, that is, by 4.6%.
