In 2025, the world is witnessing one of the largest shifts of private wealth in modern history and experiencing [millionaire migration](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/corporate-immigration-visa-services.html). Countries like the United Kingdom, China, and India are seeing record outflows of millionaires, while destinations such as the UAE, the United States, Switzerland, and parts of Southern Europe are emerging as new wealth magnets.

As wealthy people move to other countries, some nations gain new investments and opportunities, while others lose out when their rich residents leave

## **Countries Experiencing Wealth Outflows**

For many years, traditional hubs like the UK, China, and India were attractive destinations for wealthy individuals. However, changing economic conditions, higher taxes, and political uncertainty are now driving high-net-worth individuals (HNWIs) to seek better opportunities elsewhere.

According to 2025 projections, the UK is expected to record the largest millionaire outflow at 16,500, followed closely by China (15,200) and India (4,300). Other nations, including Russia and Brazil, are also seeing notable departures.

This shift highlights how global wealth is no longer tied to traditional centres but is instead moving toward regions that offer stability, investor-friendly policies, and stronger long-term growth prospects.

## **Why Millionaires Are Moving**

Millionaires are leaving some countries and moving to others because of a mix of money, safety, and lifestyle reasons:

- Countries like the UK and some EU nations have higher taxes, which can make staying expensive.

- Political changes or unstable economies make it hard to plan for the future.

- Countries like the UAE and Switzerland offer security and predictable rules.

- Business-friendly policies and incentives abroad make it easier to grow their wealth.
