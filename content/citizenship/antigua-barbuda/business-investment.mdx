### Overview

The **Business Investment** route grants Antigua & Barbuda citizenship through a **CIU-approved enterprise**. You invest, the enterprise is vetted, and jobs are planned. This path suits **builders and operators** who want economic exposure, not just an asset. Well-prepared files often complete in **~3–6 months**.

> **What qualifies?** An enterprise approved by the CIU. You may invest as a **sole investor** or join an **approved joint enterprise**.

---

### Investment Overview

**Minimum entry:** **USD 1,500,000** as a **sole investor** *or* **USD 5,000,000** for a **joint enterprise** with at least **USD 400,000 per investor**. Government fees and due diligence apply. A **5-day visit within the first 5 years** is required after citizenship.

**Ownership formats**
- **Sole investor** — direct stake and control. Clear governance and reporting lines.  
- **Joint enterprise** — pooled capital. Defined roles. CIU-approved business plan.

**Cash flow & exit**
- Returns depend on **business performance**. Dividends are **not guaranteed**.  
- Exit terms follow the **shareholders’ agreement**, CIU approval, and local law.

**Safeguards & compliance**
- CIU approval for the enterprise and capital structure.  
- Full **Source of Funds/Source of Wealth** review for all adult applicants.  
- A brief **program interview** may apply.

> **Indicative budgeting:** Plan for **investment capital** plus **government charges**, **professional fees**, and **operating reserves**.
