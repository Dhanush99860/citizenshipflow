### Overview

The **Donation Route** allows investors to secure Nauru citizenship by making a one-time, non-refundable contribution. This path is simple, fast, and suitable for investors seeking a straightforward route while including their spouse, children under 30, siblings, and parents/grandparents over 55.

### Investment Overview

- **Minimum donation:** USD 105,000 for a single applicant (varies by family size).  
- **Processing time:** Typically 3–4 months, subject to due diligence.  
- **Family inclusion:** Spouse, children under 30, siblings, and parents/grandparents over 55.  

### Benefits

- Full citizenship with **visa-free/visa-on-arrival access** to 86 countries.  
- Include eligible family members in one application.  
- No residency requirement; citizenship is lifetime and inheritable.  
- Dual citizenship permitted.  
- Safe, politically stable, and tax-friendly country in the Pacific.
