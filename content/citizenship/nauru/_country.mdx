## Nauru Citizenship by Investment — Donation Option
The **Donation Investment Option** offers a fast and straightforward path to **Nauruan citizenship**. By making a one-time, non-refundable contribution, applicants and their families can gain **lifetime citizenship**, **dual nationality**, and **visa-free travel to 86 countries**, with **no residency requirements**.
