### Overview

The PBO route offers a direct pathway to St. Kitts & Nevis citizenship via a **non-refundable contribution** to an **Approved Public Benefit Project** after Approval-in-Principle (AIP).

### Investment Overview

A **USD 250,000** contribution is required for a single applicant, with additional amounts for family members. Due-diligence, processing, interview and passport fees apply.
