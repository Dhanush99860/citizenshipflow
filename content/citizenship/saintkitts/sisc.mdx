### SISC Overview
The SISC route offers a direct pathway to St. Kitts & Nevis citizenship through a single, non-refundable contribution after receiving approval in principle (AIP).

### Investment Overview
A USD 250,000 SISC contribution is required for a single applicant, with higher totals for families. Additional due diligence and passport issuance fees apply.
