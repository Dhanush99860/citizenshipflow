## About São Tomé & Príncipe
São Tomé & Príncipe is a peaceful island nation in the Gulf of Guinea offering one of the world’s most affordable and fastest citizenship by investment programs. With no residency requirements, dual citizenship permitted, and visa-free travel benefits, it is an attractive option for investors and their families.
