### Overview

The donation route funds São Tomé & Príncipe’s **National Transformation Fund (NTF)** and offers a predictable path to citizenship without residency.

### Investment Overview

Make a **one-time NTF contribution** after approval-in-principle. Due-diligence, processing, interview (if requested) and passport fees apply per person.
