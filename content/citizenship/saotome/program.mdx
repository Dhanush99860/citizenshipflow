## Overview

São Tomé & Príncipe offers a fast and affordable Citizenship by Investment (CBI) program. Applicants can qualify by making a **non-refundable contribution to the National Transformation Fund (NTF)**. Citizenship is granted typically within 6 weeks, with no residency requirement, and is available for families, including eligible dependents.

### 1. National Transformation Fund (NTF) Contribution
- **Minimum Investment:** USD 90,000 (single applicant), USD 95,000 (family of 2–4), USD 5,000 for each additional dependent beyond 4  
- **Family Inclusion:** Spouse, children under 30, parents/grandparents over 55, siblings  
- **Processing Timeline:** 6 weeks  
- **Description:** A one-time non-refundable contribution to the government’s National Transformation Fund. This fund supports national development projects and provides the **fastest route to citizenship**. No residency is required.  
- **Additional Fees:** Government processing fees, due diligence fees, professional/legal fees apply.  

> **Note:** Citizenship is **lifetime and inheritable**, and allows visa-free/visa-on-arrival travel to 60 countries. Family members are included according to government rules and fees.
