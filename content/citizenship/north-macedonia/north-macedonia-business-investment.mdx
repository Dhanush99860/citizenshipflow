## Overview
The Business / Job-Creation Route allows investors to obtain North Macedonia citizenship by establishing a business or expanding an existing one and creating jobs. It is ideal for applicants seeking **active participation in the economy** with family inclusion under a single application.

## Investment Overview
Applicants invest a minimum of **€500,000** into a government-approved business project. The investment must create the required number of jobs (VERIFY_JOBS). Government fees and due diligence costs are **€20,000**, and proof of lawful source of funds is mandatory.

## Comparison Between Investment Options
Unlike the Development Fund route, which requires a direct contribution, this route involves **active business engagement** and job creation, offering economic involvement but longer processing timelines (**12–18 months**).

## Eligibility Criteria
- Applicant must be **18+**  
- Clean criminal record  
- Proof of legal funds  
- Medical check and insurance coverage  
- Minimum job creation (VERIFY_JOBS)  
- No language requirement for investors

## Comparison Between Investment Options

| Feature                         | Development Fund / Donation Route       | Business / Job-Creation Route           |
|---------------------------------|----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Minimum Investment               | **€200,000**                            | **€500,000**                            |
| Investment Type                  | Non-refundable government contribution  | Investment in a business to create jobs|
| Typical Processing Time          | **6–12 months**                          | **12–18 months**                        |
| Government / Due Diligence Fees | **€15,000**                              | **€20,000**                             |
| Residency Requirement            | No physical residency required           | No residency required before citizenship|
| Family Inclusion                 | Spouse + children up to **25**           | Spouse + children up to **25**          |
| Risk                             | Low – contribution only                  | Medium – business and operational risks|
| Return on Investment             | None – non-refundable                    | None guaranteed – business risk applies|
| Application Complexity           | Simple, straightforward                  | More complex – requires business plan and job creation|
| Citizenship Eligibility          | Direct via fund contribution             | Conditional upon business setup and job creation|

## Application Process
1. Confirm eligibility and select the Business / Job-Creation route.  
2. Prepare all required documents.  
3. Submit application through a licensed agent with government fees.  
4. Complete due diligence and background verification.  
5. Invest **€500,000** and create the required jobs.  
6. Receive conditional approval and citizenship documentation after compliance verification.

## Required Documents
- **Identity:** Passport, birth certificate, marriage certificate (if applicable)  
- **Financial:** Bank statements, business plan, investment proof  
- **Compliance:** Police clearance, medical certificate  

## Risks & Compliance Notes
Business investment carries **financial and operational risks**. Applicants must comply with employment laws and legal regulations. No guaranteed financial return.

## Family Inclusion

- Parents from age **55**  
- Spouse: Yes  
- Siblings: No
