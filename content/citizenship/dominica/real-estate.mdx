### Dominica CBI – Real Estate Document Checklist
To begin your Dominica Real Estate Citizenship by Investment (CBI) application, ensure you have the following key documents ready:
- Passport copy – valid for at least 6 months.
- Birth certificate – for all applicants and dependents.
- Marriage certificate – if including spouse.
- Police clearance certificate – for applicants and dependents aged 16+.
- Proof of funds / source of wealth – bank statements, asset documentation, investment records.
- Health declaration / medical certificate – confirming good health.
- Educational and professional documents – if required for verification purposes.
- Proof of residence / utility bills – to confirm current address.

### Overview
The **Dominica Real Estate Citizenship by Investment (CBI) route** allows investors to obtain **full citizenship** through a purchase of **government-approved real estate**, providing a secure, asset-backed pathway for applicants and their families.

### Investment Overview
Applicants can invest in **approved real estate projects starting from USD 200,000**, with thresholds increasing based on family inclusion. **Government fees, due diligence, and passport issuance costs** apply according to CBIU regulations.

### Why Dominica
Dominica offers one of the **Caribbean’s oldest and most trusted CBI programs**, combining **rigorous due diligence**, **efficient processing**, and **visa-free access to 140+ countries** for global mobility.

### Why Choose This Route
The real estate option provides **asset-backed investment opportunities**, family inclusion under one application, and the security of a **government-approved property**. Applicants benefit from clear investment pathways, transparent fees, and lifetime citizenship with no residency requirement.
