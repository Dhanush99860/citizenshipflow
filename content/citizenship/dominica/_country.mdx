## About Dominica
Dominica is one of the Caribbean’s most **stable and trusted destinations for Citizenship by Investment (CBI)**. The country combines a strong **due diligence framework** with reliable processing timelines, allowing investors and their families to obtain **full citizenship, a Dominican passport**, and **visa-free access to 140+ countries**.
