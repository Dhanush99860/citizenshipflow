To ensure a smooth application for **Dominica citizenship via the Economic Diversification Fund (EDF)**, applicants should prepare the following **basic documents**:

### Overview of Dominica EDF Citizenship
The **Economic Diversification Fund (EDF) pathway** allows investors to obtain **Dominica citizenship** through a **non-refundable cash contribution**. The process is efficient, secure, and includes thorough due diligence to ensure a smooth application.

### What is the Economic Diversification Fund (EDF)?
Applicants can make an **EDF contribution starting from USD 100,000** for a single applicant. Contribution amounts increase with **family inclusion**, covering spouse, children, and in some cases, dependent parents. Government fees, due diligence, and passport issuance fees are applied according to official CBIU rules.

## Why Choose Dominica EDF Citizenship?
Dominica offers one of the **Caribbean’s oldest and most trusted Citizenship by Investment programs**. Benefits include **visa-free access to 140+ countries**, **lifetime citizenship**, and no residency requirements, making it ideal for global mobility and family security.

### Key Advantages
- **Fast and transparent process**: Citizenship granted typically within 3–6 months.  
- **Family-friendly**: Include spouse, children, and dependent parents in one application.  
- **Economic impact**: Contribution supports Dominica’s national development projects.  
- **Global mobility**: Visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to over 140 countries, including the Schengen Area, UK, and Hong Kong.
