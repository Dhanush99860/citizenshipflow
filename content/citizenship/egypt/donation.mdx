### Overview
The Donation Route is Egypt’s most direct path to citizenship, offering a fast and accessible option for individuals and families.

### Investment Overview
Make a one-time non-refundable donation to the state treasury after approval-in-principle. Due-diligence, processing, interview, and passport fees apply per person.
