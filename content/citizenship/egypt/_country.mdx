  Egypt’s Citizenship by Investment program, launched in 2020, provides a fast and accessible path to citizenship. Applicants can qualify via a non-refundable donation to the state treasury, investment in government-approved real estate, business investment, or a bank deposit. Processing typically takes 6–9 months, subject to background checks.

## About Egypt
Strategically located at the crossroads of Africa, Asia, and the Middle East, Egypt offers investors a fast and accessible path to citizenship with family inclusion and a minimum investment of USD 250,000.
