## Overview
The Monaco Residency by Property Investment program allows investors to obtain full residency in Monaco through property acquisition or long-term rental. Eligible investors must maintain a minimum bank deposit of **€500,000** alongside their property investment. Family members can be included, and residents enjoy no personal income tax and Schengen visa-free travel.

### Investment Overview
Investors can purchase luxury apartments starting at **€500,000**, high-end villas from **€1,000,000**, or commercial properties suitable for business purposes. Funds must be deposited in a Monaco bank account and property ownership or rental agreements must be secured.
