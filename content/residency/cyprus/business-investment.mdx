## Overview
The Cyprus Golden Visa Business Investment route allows non-EU nationals to obtain permanent residency by investing in a Cyprus-based company. Eligible companies must employ at least **5** full-time staff. Dependents including spouse, children under **25**, and parents are included, with minimal presence required – one visit every **two years**.

### Investment Overview
Investors can inject a minimum of **€300,000** into an operational Cyprus-based company. The company must employ at least **five** full-time staff members. Funds must be transferred from abroad and comply with Cyprus corporate regulations.
