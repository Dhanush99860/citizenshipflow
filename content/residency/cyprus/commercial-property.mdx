### Overview
The Cyprus Golden Visa Commercial Property route allows non-EU nationals to obtain permanent residency by investing in approved commercial properties. Eligible properties include offices, shops, hotels, or similar developments. Dependents including spouse, children under **25**, and parents are included, with minimal presence required – one visit every **two years**.

### Investment Overview
Investors may purchase up to **two** commercial properties from approved developers. The minimum investment is **€300,000** (excluding VAT). Funds must be transferred from abroad and properties must comply with Cyprus regulations.
