## Overview
The **Cyprus Golden Visa – Fund Investment** route enables non-EU investors to secure **long-term permanent residency** by subscribing **EUR 300,000** to **regulated collective investment schemes** registered in Cyprus. Eligible **dependents** — spouse, children under **25**, and parents — can be included in the same application. Ongoing maintenance is simple: visit Cyprus **once every two years** to keep status active.

## Investment Overview
Applicants invest a minimum of **EUR 300,000** in approved collective investment schemes, meeting program regulations and due-diligence standards. Funds must originate from **lawful sources abroad**. The program grants **residency rights without local employment**, while dependents benefit from access to **healthcare** and **education**.
