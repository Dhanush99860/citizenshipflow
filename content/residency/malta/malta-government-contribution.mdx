## Overview
The Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP) via the **Government Contribution Route** allows non-EU, non-EEA, and non-Swiss nationals to obtain permanent residence by making the mandatory government contribution and an NGO donation. This route does **not** require property ownership or lease, providing flexibility and fast access to Malta’s Schengen privileges. The program covers family members including spouse, dependent children, and eligible parents or grandparents, without a minimum stay requirement.

## Investment Overview
- **Government Contribution:** €37,000 (non-refundable)  
- **NGO Donation:** €2,000 (non-refundable)  
- **Property:** Not required  
- **Family:** Spouse, dependent children (to 26), and eligible parents/grandparents
