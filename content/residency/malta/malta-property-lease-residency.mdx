## Overview
The **Malta Permanent Residence – Property Lease Route** allows investors from outside the EU, EEA, and Switzerland to obtain permanent residence by leasing a residential property. This route provides flexibility, lower upfront investment, and immediate access to Malta’s stable lifestyle, healthcare, and education. Family members including spouse, children, parents, and grandparents can be included. No minimum stay is required.

## Investment Overview
Applicants must lease a qualifying residential property for at least **5 years**. The **minimum annual lease** is **€14,000** in standard regions and **€10,000** in Gozo or southern Malta. A **government contribution of €37,000** and **NGO donation of €2,000** are required. Proof of assets **≥ €500,000**, including at least **€150,000 in liquid instruments**, is mandatory.
