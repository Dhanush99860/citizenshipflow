### Overview

Subclass 189 is a **points-tested permanent residence** visa for invited workers. You must submit an **EOI in SkillSelect**, meet the **65-point pass mark**, be **under 45 at invitation**, hold a **positive skills assessment**, and meet at least **Competent English**.

### Salary overview

There is **no minimum salary requirement tied to visa grant** for 189 and **no sponsoring employer**. After PR, employment must comply with Australian workplace laws and market rates. You may work for any employer in any state/territory.

### Why Australia (189)

- PR on grant; live and work anywhere  
- No sponsor or state nomination required  
- Transparent points system and published invitation activity

### Comparison

**189 vs 190 (state nominated)** — 189 has no nomination obligations but is often more competitive on points. **190** grants PR as well and nomination provides **+5 points**, but you must meet state criteria and settlement expectations.

### Notes on competitiveness

Invitation volumes and occupational ceilings fluctuate. Higher points, recent relevant experience and stronger English typically improve competitiveness.
