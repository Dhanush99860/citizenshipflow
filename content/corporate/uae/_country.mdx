### Overview

The UAE streamlines **company setup** and **employer-sponsored residence**. Choose **free zone** (100% foreign ownership) or **mainland** (onshore reach), then proceed to **establishment card**, **entry permit**, **medical**, **Emirates ID**, and **residence visa**.

### Why UAE?

- Strategic base with pro-business regulation  
- Fast incorporation and predictable immigration steps  
- Access to regional markets and talent  
- High quality infrastructure, schooling, and banking

### Key terms

- **Establishment Card:** Enables a UAE entity to sponsor visas  
- **Entry Permit:** Electronic pre-approval prior to status change/entry  
- **Status Change:** In-country conversion of status without exit  
- **Emirates ID:** Biometric national ID required for services
