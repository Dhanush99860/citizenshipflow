### Overview

Set up a **Dubai free zone company** to unlock **employer-sponsored residence visas** for founders and staff. After incorporation and the **establishment card**, file **entry permits**, complete **status change**, **medical**, and **Emirates ID**, then receive a **digital residence visa**. Most zones offer **flexi-desk** options and streamlined renewals.

### Benefits

- 100% foreign ownership and straightforward renewals  
- Employer sponsorship for founders and staff  
- Digital, trackable immigration milestones  
- Access to banking, utilities, and dependants post-visa

### Eligibility

- Company incorporated with valid **license** and **establishment card**  
- Role/position satisfies **zone salary/level** criteria  
- Clean **medical** and compliance with immigration checks  
- Complete **KYC** and attested documents where requested

### Documents

- Passports, photos, and address proofs  
- Company license, MoA, establishment card  
- Education/professional certificates (if required)  
- Entry permit, medical results, Emirates ID receipt

### Process (at a glance)

1) Choose zone & activity → 2) Incorporation & license → 3) Establishment card & e-channels → 4) Entry permit → 5) Status change/Entry → 6) Medical & Emirates ID → 7) Residence visa issuance

### Comparison

**Free Zone vs Mainland:** Free zones emphasise simplified administration and sector-focused ecosystems. **Mainland** enables onshore trading and broader activity codes but different compliance. Your customer base and activity determine fit.

### Why UAE

- Regional hub with strong banking and logistics  
- Visa pathways integrated with company setup  
- Mature free-zone ecosystem and service infrastructure

### Not a fit?

- Highly regulated activities without special approvals  
- Entities unwilling to meet ongoing **license renewals** and **KYC**  
- Applicants unable to pass medical or provide required attestations
